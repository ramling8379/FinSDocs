Privacy Policy for FinS Mobile App

Effective Date: 2026-09-14

FinS Mobile App ("FinS", "we", "our", or "us") helps users record and manage loan-related information, including loan amounts, EMI payment status, collections, and profit or loss summaries. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the limits of the app's role.

1. Purpose of the App

FinS is a record-keeping and calculation tool. The app allows users to enter and track loan-related information based on their own inputs. FinS does not own, issue, authorize, verify, guarantee, or collect any loan on behalf of users or any third party.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information that you provide directly:

  • Account information such as your email address
  • Loan-related records entered by you
  • EMI payment details, including paid and unpaid amounts
  • Collection records
  • Profit and loss summary data generated from your entries

The app works primarily on user-provided information. Because of this, the accuracy of records depends entirely on what users enter.

3. Passwords and Account Security

We do not read, store, or log your password in plain text in our database. Password-related information is handled using secure encrypted or hashed methods as part of authentication systems.

If you forget your password, you may reset it using the "Forgot Password" functionality. To do so, you must provide a valid email address associated with your account.

4. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Store the financial and loan records you enter
  • Display loan summaries, EMI tracking, collection summaries, and profit or loss calculations
  • Support account recovery and basic app operations

5. Accuracy of Data

FinS depends on user input. We do not independently verify whether the information entered into the app is complete, accurate, or current. Users are solely responsible for reviewing and confirming the records they maintain in the app.

6. No Legal Authority or Evidence

FinS is only a tool for logging and organizing financial records. Information stored in the app is based on user input and must not be treated as legal proof, legal evidence, or an official statement of rights or obligations between parties.

Any dispute, agreement, repayment obligation, or legal relationship between lenders, borrowers, customers, or other parties remains solely between those parties. FinS is not responsible for those relationships or outcomes.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with third parties except:

  • when needed to operate core app services such as authentication or password reset
  • when required by law, regulation, court order, or valid legal process
  • when necessary to protect our rights, users, or the security of the app

8. Data Retention

We retain account and app data for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account access, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

9. User Responsibility

You are responsible for:

  • entering accurate information
  • maintaining the confidentiality of your account access
  • using the app only for lawful purposes
  • keeping separate original records if you need documentation for legal or official use

10. Limitation of Responsibility

FinS provides record-keeping and calculation support only. We are not liable for losses, disputes, missed collections, incorrect entries, legal claims, or decisions made based on data entered by users into the app.

11. Children's Privacy

FinS is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use such services without consent. If we learn that personal information has been collected in violation of applicable law, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the FinS Mobile App support team through the contact details provided in the app or its official listing.
